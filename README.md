**Zhannur-Home page**
This webpage is designed for a travel agency called Tripify, featuring a fixed navigation bar with links to various sections like Home, Destinations, and Booking. It includes engaging elements like a top banner, a progress bar, and a stats section displaying the number of happy travelers. Three travel experience cards (Beach Paradise, Mountain Adventure, Safari Tour) are featured, each with booking options and ratings. The page also highlights services such as Tour Packages, Booking, and Special Offers, with dynamic elements like a "Read More" button and a random travel fact feature. The layout is responsive, ensuring a smooth user experience across devices, and it uses Bootstrap, jQuery, and FontAwesome for styling and interactivity.

**Destination page**-This webpage showcases popular travel destinations offered by the Tripify travel agency. It features a fixed navigation bar, a current weather section for various cities, and a display of popular destinations like Paris, Tokyo, Dubai, New York, and more, with cards showing images and names of each city. The page also includes a "Show Time" button to display the current time and a footer with copyright information and social media links. The layout is responsive, utilizing Bootstrap for seamless viewing across different devices.

**Symbat-Packages page**
This webpage is dedicated to showcasing various tour packages offered by the Tripify travel agency. The page includes a fixed header with navigation links to various sections like Home, Destinations, Packages, and more, as well as a language selector for English, Russian, and Kazakh. The sidebar lists categories of tours such as Family Tours, Romantic Tours, Beach Tours, and Cultural Tours, with each category containing descriptions of the offerings. The main content section displays various tour packages with images, brief descriptions, and pricing information, allowing users to browse through family, romantic, beach, and cultural tours. A search bar is provided for users to find specific tour packages, and the footer contains copyright details and social media links.

**Special Offers page**
This webpage presents exclusive special offers from the Tripify travel agency, highlighting discounts of up to 70%. It includes a fixed header with navigation links to different sections like Home, Destinations, Packages, and more. The main content area displays several travel offers with images, brief descriptions, original and discounted prices, and a "Book Now" button for each package. The offers include trips to destinations like Antalya, Dubai, Maldives, and Paris, with details such as departure dates and trip durations. The footer contains copyright information, team member credits, and social media links for user engagement.

**Gulnaz-Guide page**
Guide Page: The "Guide" page offers essential travel information like visa and document requirements, language tips, local culture, and travel lifehacks. It includes helpful categories like Visas & Documents, Language Tips, Local Culture, and Travel Lifehacks, presented with clear, visually appealing cards. Additionally, there is a "Tip of the Day" section offering practical advice for travelers

**Support Page**
The Support page is designed to assist users with any travel-related concerns. It provides 24/7 emergency support for urgent issues like lost documents or travel emergencies, with a dedicated phone number for immediate assistance. In addition to emergency help, the page also offers guidance for general inquiries, including assistance with bookings, cancellations, and modifications. The page further includes a FAQ & Resources section, covering topics like booking help, payment issues, travel documents, and cancellation policies.
For users who still need help, there is a contact form where they can send a message, including their name, email, subject, and a detailed message about their issue. The form ensures that users can expect a response within 24 hours. The page also features a success alert that appears when a message is successfully sent, providing reassurance that the issue will be addressed soon. The layout is user-friendly and designed to ensure that customers can easily find the help they need.

**Ayazhan-Booking page**
This webpage allows users to book their trips through the Tripify travel agency. The page features a well-structured form where users can provide their name, email, destination, travel date, and number of travelers. A destination modal helps users choose from a list of popular destinations such as Tokyo, Sydney, Dubai, and more. The form also includes a discount feature based on the number of travelers, with discounts of up to 20%. The page offers a theme toggle to switch between light and dark modes, and users can copy their booking details to the clipboard with a button. The form validates inputs and plays success/error sounds based on form submission.

**About us Page**
This webpage is the "About Us" page for Tripify, a travel agency, featuring several key sections. The navigation bar includes a theme toggle for switching between light and dark modes, as well as links to various pages like Home, Destinations, Packages, and more. The main content area introduces Tripify, showcases the team with profile cards for each member, and provides a search bar to filter through a FAQ section. The FAQ section answers common questions like services offered, booking modifications, and group discounts, with collapsible answers for better user interaction. Additionally, the page displays the current date and time, and a footer includes copyright information and social media links.
